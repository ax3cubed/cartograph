# Code of Conduct

Cartograph adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies to this project. This page is a short summary.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. People arrive with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give feedback on the work. Leave the person out of it.
- Accept feedback on your own work in good faith.
- Assume good intent, and say so plainly when something bothers you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, reviews and any other channel used for Cartograph. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, using the contact details on https://github.com/ax3cubed. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may block a person from the project.
